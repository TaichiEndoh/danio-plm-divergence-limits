# Scope and unresolved limitations

1. This is an exploratory comparison of model concordance within selected Danio pairs, not an experimentally validated functional predictor.
2. Primary: 2,662 pairs with recorded lengths no greater than 2,046 residues; full selected union: 3,428. Recorded baseline: 50,921 distinct pairs. The 165-pair difference from the earlier reported 51,086 set remains unresolved; no filtering explanation is asserted.
3. Six unresolved AHR2 residues and their relationship to high local distances remain material limitations. Matching coordinates does not resolve their biological identity.
4. Corrected full-width ESM-2 windows and ESM-3 residue representations use different contexts. Near-terminal and gapped positions require the documented exclusions. Legacy file names containing “rediscovery” or “grounding” do not establish functional validation.
5. Archived variant-percentile summaries are provided, but complete raw L×19 distributions are missing. Their percentiles cannot be independently recomputed from these summaries.
6. The original model-weight/input/configuration provenance is incomplete. Current files and vectors are fingerprinted; present-day hashes do not retroactively authenticate past inference.
7. Complete historical annotation snapshots and some original accession versions are unavailable. The retained intervals and sequence bytes are the reproducible objects.
8. Structures are predictions; structural deviation and pLDDT are not experimental truth. Low pLDDT alone does not prove disorder.
9. The release verifier checks saved-output consistency. It does not establish that every possible bug has been excluded or that all scientific claims have been independently validated.

The original research repository, grant materials, correspondence, current AhR third-paper analyses, manuscript personal contact details and model weights are excluded from this submission snapshot.
